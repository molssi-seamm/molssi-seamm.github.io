Phase 7: the dielectric constant and the ionic conductivity#

Status: done (2026-10-17). The library module seamm_md_analysis.electric was released in seamm-md-analysis 2026.10.16.1, the kspace style in md.h5md in lammps-step 2026.10.16.4, and the two steps as dielectric-constant-step and ionic-conductivity-step 2026.10.17 (Paul accepted the five recommendations in section 6 on 2026-10-16). The validation is in section 7.

Goal#

The static dielectric constant of liquids and of the solvent in electrolyte solutions, and the ionic conductivity of electrolytes, from equilibrium MD, with errors, graphs and stored results – what science now computes in scripts (mlff_dipoles.py, eps_both.py; campaign 2026-10-16, section 3.4) for classical forcefields.

The design document’s table says: “Dielectric constant: our own code. MDAnalysis’s DielectricConstant assumes no free charges, so it cannot be used for electrolytes. For an electrolyte, use the dipole of the neutral molecules or separate out the ionic current; which one is to be settled in that phase.” Ionic conductivity (Nernst–Einstein and the full Einstein form, as in mdgo) follows.

1. The physics#

Dielectric constant. With Ewald or PPPM electrostatics and conducting (“tin-foil”) boundary conditions, as LAMMPS uses, the static dielectric constant of a periodic cell of volume V at temperature T is

\[\varepsilon = 1 + \frac{\langle \mathbf{M}^2 \rangle - \langle \mathbf{M} \rangle^2} {3 V \varepsilon_0 k_B T}\]

with M the dipole of the cell, Σ qᵢ rᵢ over the atoms of whole molecules. It converges slowly – the dipole decorrelates over picoseconds and its fluctuations need nanoseconds – so the errors come from blocks or independent runs, and a graph of the running estimate shows the convergence.

  • A neutral liquid (water, carbonates): M over every molecule.

  • An electrolyte: the ions carry a current, not a bounded dipole, so M over the whole cell grows without limit. The step uses the dipole of the neutral molecules only, which gives the dielectric constant of the solvent in the presence of the ions – the quantity usually reported for electrolyte solutions (the dielectric decrement). It leaves out the ion-solvent cross term, which a rigorous treatment separating the ionic current would include; that is a later option (see Decisions).

  • Molecular ions (BF4⁻, PF6⁻) are ions: they are left out of M like monatomic ones. Molecules are classified by their total charge; one that is neither neutral nor an integer charge (a model artefact) is reported.

Ionic conductivity.

  • Nernst–Einstein, from the self-diffusion coefficients of the ions: σ_NE = e² / (V k_B T) Σ_species N_s z_s² D_s.

  • Einstein–Helfand, from the collective displacement of the charges, σ = lim_{t→∞} ⟨‖Σᵢ qᵢ (rᵢ(t) − rᵢ(0))‖²⟩ / (6 V k_B t), which includes the correlations between ions (ion pairing lowers it).

  • Their ratio, the ionicity σ/σ_NE.

The collective form is noisy; it needs long runs and several runs or blocks, as for the viscosity. The Yeh–Hummer finite-size correction applies to the self-diffusion coefficients (it can use the viscosity from a Viscosity step, as Diffusivity does).

2. Where the dipole and the displacements come from#

From the trajectory, not from sampling in LAMMPS: the positions in md.h5md are unwrapped, so molecules stay whole, and the forcefield charges are stored there too. The same frames give M(t) and the ions’ displacements, for any MD code that writes the file, with no change to the LAMMPS step. Frames every 0.1-0.5 ps are enough (the step asks for them in mode A and checks for them in mode B, as the other analysis steps do); the dipole decorrelates over picoseconds.

Charges. Only fixed charges, from the forcefield, are supported. A machine-learned forcefield has no fixed charges in the file, so the steps refuse MLFF runs with a clear message. Charges from the MLFF engine (over MDI) wait on whether the LES models’ latent charges give usable dipoles (science, 2026-10-14).

Electrostatics. The formula above holds for Ewald or PPPM with tin-foil boundary conditions. The LAMMPS H5MD writer records the kspace style in the run’s parameters, and the step warns when it is anything else (a cutoff or reaction-field treatment needs a different formula).

3. The steps#

Two steps, built like Viscosity and Liquid Structure (their own MD as replicas, or the MD before them), sharing a new library module:

  • Dielectric Constant: ε with its error, the running estimate, the autocorrelation of M and its correlation time; for an electrolyte, which molecules made up M. Results: ε, its error, and for each run the volume, temperature and ⟨M²⟩.

  • Ionic Conductivity: σ (Einstein–Helfand) and σ_NE with their errors, the ionicity, the self-diffusion coefficient of each ion (with Yeh–Hummer when a viscosity is available), the Helfand moment and MSD graphs. Results in S/m.

Both need a periodic system and fixed charges; both check, before the MD runs, that positions are sampled often enough.

4. The library: seamm_md_analysis.electric#

  • molecule_charges(configuration) and the classification (neutral molecules, ions, others);

  • cell_dipole(frames, atoms, charges): M(t) from positions, streamed;

  • dielectric_constant(M, V, T, blocks): ε, its error, the running estimate;

  • charge_displacement(frames, ions, charges): the collective translational dipole of the ions, for the Helfand moment; the existing fit_helfand_moment and msd_by_species do the rest;

  • nernst_einstein(D, counts, charges, V, T).

Released first, then the two steps, as in the earlier phases.

5. Tests and validation#

  • Synthetic, with known answers: N fixed dipoles of moment μ oriented at random each frame (⟨M²⟩ = Nμ², so ε is known); independent random walks of charges (σ = σ_NE exactly, ionicity 1); perfectly paired ions moving together (σ = 0).

  • Charges: neutral molecules, monatomic and molecular ions, and a non-integer molecule reported.

  • H5MD round trip from the LAMMPS writer, including the recorded kspace style.

  • GUI over every layout; run path with a small LAMMPS run locally.

  • Real data: SPC/E water against the published value for the model (about 70); 1 molal NaCl in SPC/E for the dielectric decrement and the conductivity, against published values for the same models and science’s eps_both.py.

6. Decisions (Paul, 2026-10-16: the recommendations below)#

  1. Electrolytes: the dipole of the neutral molecules (recommended), with a later option to separate the ionic current rigorously (including the ion-solvent cross term) – or do the rigorous treatment now?

  2. The dipole from the trajectory (recommended), rather than sampling it in LAMMPS (compute dipole), which would need an MD-step change and a group of the neutral molecules.

  3. Two steps, Dielectric Constant and Ionic Conductivity (recommended, in line with one analysis per step), or one step with both?

  4. MLFFs refused until their engines supply charges (recommended), rather than accepting per-species charges given by the user.

  5. Nernst–Einstein from the step’s own MSD of the ions (recommended: one step, one run, consistent frames), or from a preceding Diffusivity step’s results?

7. Validation (2026-10-16/17)#

Run through the JobServer on the development installation (project Validation), with five independent 2 ns NVT runs at 298 K after 100 ps of equilibration, positions every 0.5 ps, PPPM:

  • SPC/E water (216 molecules), mode A: ε = 72.4 ± 1.9 and a dipole correlation time of 10.1 ps, in line with the published value for the model (about 70).

  • 1 molal NaCl in SPC/E (9 ion pairs, 512 waters), the MD in a loop and both steps analysing “the last MD run of each loop iteration”:

    • ε of the water 57.3 ± 1.4 (correlation time 11.7 ps): the dielectric decrement of about 15 expected at 1 m;

    • σ (Einstein–Helfand) 3.1 ± 0.9 S/m, σ_NE 6.3 S/m, ionicity 0.49;

    • D(Na⁺) 0.69 ± 0.12 and D(Cl⁻) 1.06 ± 0.10 × 10⁻⁹ m²/s, without the Yeh–Hummer correction (about +0.33 × 10⁻⁹ m²/s for this cell).

    The ionicity looks low and σ has a large error: the collective MSD needs longer or more runs than five of 2 ns.

The first NaCl job found a bug: in a loop, a step’s name includes the iteration’s directory (4.iter_2.0.4), so only the last iteration’s run was analysed. It was fixed in seamm-md-analysis 2026.10.17, and the results above are from a re-analysis of the same trajectories with the fixed version.
